Twitch

Create an app at dev.twitch.tv/console/apps — category Broadcasting Suite, OAuth Redirect URL https://localhost:7334/twitch/callback. Your channel must be at least Affiliate for bits & subs.

That redirect URL is HTTPS on a self-signed local certificate (Twitch requires HTTPS even for localhost). The first time it's hit — right after you click Authorize below — your browser will say the connection isn't private. That's expected: click Advanced → Proceed to localhost. It only happens once.
